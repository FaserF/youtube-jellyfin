🎵 YouTube Music Integration (Optional — Enabled by Default)

Integrates YouTube Music as both a native Jellyfin Music Channel and a dedicated Jellyfin Music Library with audio-only HQ streaming (AAC/M4A), mapped automatically to the primary account and optionally available to other users via Public Mode or self-service TV Code login.

🎬 Movie & TV Series Trailers (Emby Cinema Mode Parity)

Provides native, 1-click YouTube trailer playback for all Movies and TV Series in Jellyfin libraries. Automatically creates lightweight companion -trailer.strm files and resolves TMDb remote trailers anonymously as guest with zero user-account scrobbling or history pollution.
